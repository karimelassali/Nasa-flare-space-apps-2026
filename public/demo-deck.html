01 · The problem

Fire in space does not behave like fire on Earth.

With little or no gravity to drive buoyant flow, flames in microgravity can be cooler, rounder, slower to spread — and at times unexpectedly persistent. On the ISS, and on future Moon and Mars missions where evacuation is impossible, a fire can become life-threatening in minutes.

  • Decades of NASA experiments — BASS, Saffire, SoFIE, FLEX, ACME — hold the evidence.
  • That evidence lives in the PSI Data Repository: hundreds of investigations, hard to search and compare.
  • Scientists need a tool that turns those experiments into clear, usable fire-safety insight.

Slide 2 of 7 · Sources: NASA Space Apps Challenge, “Flame in Freefall”; PSI Data Repository (psi.nasa.gov)

02 · What FLARE is

One workspace. Seven ways to explore the evidence.

FLARE is an SI-powered, web-based dashboard that summarizes, ranks, and interprets NASA microgravity combustion experiments by flammability, data availability, and experimental relevance.

  • Data from the PSI Data Repository, enriched with NASA Technical Reports Server (NTRS) publications.

03 · The AI feature

Ask in plain language. Get a record back.

FLARE’s AI feature is the Research Agent: natural-language queries over the NASA corpus. Ask “Compare SAFFIRE-I and SAFFIRE-III” and it selects the relevant records and measurements — with the source behind every number.

  • The model only selects existing record IDs and measurement keys. Scientific text is rendered deterministically from stored source fields — the model cannot invent a number.
  • Unknown citations or extra prose are rejected by a strict schema.
  • With no AI key configured, FLARE falls back to deterministic retrieval and says so on screen. The same evidence stays available.
  • Creative requests outside the corpus (poems, stories) are refused with a guide answer — this is a research tool, not a chatbot.

Slide 4 of 7 · Providers: NVIDIA NIM, Google Gemini, OpenAI (server-side keys only)

05 · Honest limits

FLARE shows where the evidence stops.

  • Overview records do not supply missing measurements — FLARE never fills them in.
  • SAFFIRE-V publications report different atmosphere values for Sample V-4. FLARE shows them separately, not merged.
  • The 3D Simulation Lab is a conceptual illustration of chamber geometry — not CFD, not a measured flame shape, not a fire detector.
  • FLARE is a research and education tool. It is not flight hardware, not certified for onboard fire-safety decisions, and not endorsed by NASA.
